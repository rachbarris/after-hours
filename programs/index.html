Our programs

Two ways to learn with us

The Cohort Series

Serious, text-based learning over four to six weeks.

The cohort learning experience we offer is truly unique. It gives busy women the opportunity to engage in serious, text-based Torah learning over an extended period of time, in a way that actually feels manageable to commit to. Each series runs about four to six weeks, long enough to really get into a topic and cover meaningful ground. And because the group is small, the experience feels personal. We plan to offer a new cohort each season.

Sponsor a cohort

The Moonlight Shift

A monthly evening of Torah in honor of Rosh Chodesh.

We designed The Moonlight Shift for women who want more Torah in their lives but can’t realistically commit to a full six-week cohort. Once a month, in honor of Rosh Chodesh, it offers a way to make Torah learning a regular part of life without adding another major commitment to the calendar. The teachers are incredible and the topics are carefully chosen. We know that fifty minutes on a weeknight is precious. We always make it worth it.

The Moonlight Shift is free and open to all who are interested.

Time: 8:00pm-8:50pm (home by 9!)
Where: Address shared upon RSVP
